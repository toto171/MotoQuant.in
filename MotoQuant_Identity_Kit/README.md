# MotoQuant Identity Kit

## The mark — "the redline Q"
A heavyweight dial ring (the Q / a tachometer) with a sector gap at 315°.
A single tapered needle launches from the hub, accelerates outward
(reverse taper = gaining width = gaining speed) and exits the dial through
the gap with a 45° speed cut. The needle swept past redline and left the
instrument — the physics of faster.

The Q doubles as: Quant / quarter-mile / the dial in the product's own UI.

## Construction
- Ring stroke = 30% of outer radius. Gap = 30° sector centered at 315°.
- Slash axis = 315° exactly. Widths: 0.34→0.95 of ring stroke.
- Type: Space Grotesk 700 (converted to outlines — no font dependency).
  Tagline: JetBrains Mono 500, +0.30–0.46em tracking.
  Both are the product's own display/label faces, so web, app, and logo speak
  one voice.

## Color
- Ink        #0A0A0B  (backgrounds)
- Signal     #FF5E1F  (the slash, "QUANT", never for body text)
- White      #FFFFFF
Rule: orange is reserved for the moving part(s) — slash, hub, QUANT.
Everything static is white or ink.

Keep a margin of at least the ring stroke width (0.3R) around the lockup.
Don't rotate the mark; the 315° axis is the identity.
