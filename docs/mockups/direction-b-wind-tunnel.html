Real dyno curves — not multiplier estimates.

Every catalog bike's torque curve is either measured (where instrumented data exists) or synthesised from a Gaussian expansion model with category-specific tuning. The curve traces in real time as the bike sweeps through its rev range.

For two-strokes, the expansion-chamber resonance at tuned_rpm creates the characteristic narrow band — we model it explicitly rather than smoothing it away.
