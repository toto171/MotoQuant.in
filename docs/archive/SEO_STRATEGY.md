## Technical SEO — What Was Implemented

### ✅ Done (April 2026)

1. **SoftwareApplication JSON-LD** in layout.js
   - Tells Google exactly what MotoQuant Pro is, its features, pricing (free), and rating
   - Eligible for rich results in SERPs

2. **FAQPage JSON-LD** in page.js
   - 12 carefully chosen FAQs targeting real queries
   - Eligible for "People Also Ask" expansion in search results — this is significant
   - Key targeted: "[Bike] quarter mile time", "altitude effect on drag racing", "best mods for drag racing india"

3. **ItemList JSON-LD** on /bikes
   - 108 bikes as structured list — Google can show these in rich results

4. **Full meta tag overhaul** (title, description, keywords, OG, Twitter card, canonical)
   - Title now includes "Motorcycle Quarter Mile Simulator & ET Predictor" — actual search terms
   - OG locale set to en_IN — signals India relevance to Google

5. **sitemap.xml** auto-generated by Next.js
   - Covers /, /bikes, and all 108 /bikes/[slug] pages
   - Priority and changeFrequency set correctly

6. **robots.txt** — clean crawl signals, sitemap reference

7. **/bikes page** — 108-bike index with:
   - H1: "Motorcycle Quarter Mile Times" (exact search query)
   - Per-bike structured data (cc, PS, kg, ET, trap speed)
   - Filtered by manufacturer — Google loves taxonomy pages
   - Internal links to individual bike pages

8. **Footer keyword text** — subtle, non-spammy keyword reinforcement for long-tail crawling

9. **H1 updated** on homepage — now says "Motorcycle Quarter Mile Simulator" instead of "The Physics of Faster" (tagline moved to subtitle)
