## Hypothesis

The blog index renders 22 post cards in a 3-column grid and then ends. Visitors who scroll the full grid are high-intent — they came to learn drag racing physics, they read the meta blurb, they scanned 22 titles. But the page offers no terminal action: if no title grabbed them, they bounce.

A grid-aligned CTA card pinned after the last post — copy: *"Stop reading. Run the numbers on your bike."* — should convert the otherwise-bounced cohort into `/simulator` runs. The bounce cohort is the target; this is not trying to steal clicks from post cards.

Single-variable test: the only thing changing is **presence of the end-of-grid CTA card**. Copy and href are fixed across the variant arm so we cannot confound placement with messaging in a follow-up.

## Variants

| Arm | Description |
|---|---|
| **A (control, default)** | No terminal CTA. Post grid ends, footer follows. Pixel-identical to the pre-EXP-006 page. |

## Primary metric

`simulator_runs_per_blog_index_visitor` — number of `/simulator` page hits originating from a `/blog` session, divided by total `/blog` visitors per arm.

## Secondary metrics

- `blog_index_bounce_rate` — should not increase on B (CTA must not look out of place).
- `blog_post_click_through_rate` — should not drop materially on B (we want net new conversions, not cannibalised post clicks).
- `scroll_depth_pct` — confirms visitors are actually seeing the CTA before measuring conversion lift.
- `blog_index_to_simulator_ctr` — direct CTR on the CTA itself.
- `blog_index_to_pricing_ctr` — sanity check that the CTA is not redirecting traffic that would have flowed to /pricing.

## Min sample

200 per arm. No decision before that.

## Decision criteria

**Ship variant B as new control if:**
- `simulator_runs/blog_index_visitor` lifts ≥15% with p<0.05 over ≥200 samples per arm, **AND**
- `blog_post_click_through_rate` does not drop >10% (we want net new conversions, not cannibalisation — if visitors abandon post reads entirely for the CTA, the blog stops doing its SEO job).

**Roll back if:**
- `blog_index_bounce_rate` rises >10% (CTA looks pushy or out of place), or
- `scroll_depth_pct` drops materially (visitors see the CTA early and exit before engaging the grid).

If B wins, evaluate replicating the same pattern on `/features` and `/docs` index pages as separate follow-up micro-experiments — one page at a time, not a blanket rollout. Different page intents may produce different lifts; each replication is its own validation.

## SEO safety

- Same canonical URL (`/blog`).
- Same h1, same meta description, same OG tags.
- `page.js` stays a server component — `EndOfBlogIndexCta` is the only client island, wrapped in `<Suspense fallback={null}>` per the Vercel static-prerender rule (`useSearchParams()` requires a Suspense boundary or the page fails to prerender).
- Variant A renders a hidden `<span>` placeholder so the DOM tree is byte-equivalent to the pre-experiment control. Variant B injects a single block-level card that does not affect grid layout (lives below the grid, not inside it).
- No change to the 22 post cards, their titles, slugs, tags, or excerpts — keyword density preserved.

## Analytics attribution

- `data-exp="EXP-006"` + `data-variant` on the wrapper div and the inner Link.
- `data-cta="end-of-blog-index"` on the Link for click-source disambiguation against other simulator CTAs across the site.

## Expected analysis date

2026-06-12 (assumes ~10 visitors/day to `/blog` based on current Vercel edge counts; 200 samples per arm ≈ 20 days of organic traffic at current scale). If traffic ramps via SEO or referral the analysis window collapses accordingly.

## Kill criteria

If after 30 days neither arm has reached 200 samples, the experiment is traffic-bound rather than effect-bound — promote a different blog index improvement (e.g. category-filter chips, or sort-by-recency toggle) and shelve EXP-006 until blog traffic grows enough to support the test.
