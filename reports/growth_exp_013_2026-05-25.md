# EXP-013 — /whitepaper terminal CTA copy retest

- **Started:** 2026-05-25
- **Page:** `/whitepaper`
- **Element:** terminal third-card CTA copy (button label + subhead) in the
  Continue-reading footer (the orange-bordered card adjacent to Physics docs /
  Validation data).
- **Status:** live (default A, opt-in B via `?variant=B`)
- **Flag:** `?variant=B`

## Hypothesis

Whitepaper readers who reach the Continue-reading footer have absorbed 10
sections of methodology — abstract, architecture, the 8-cluster taxonomy,
the 74-benchmark validation table, K5/R1/Hayabusa reference baselines,
reproducibility, limitations, roadmap, citation. They sit at peak technical
credibility but the current terminal CTA describes the mechanism ("Run a
simulation" + "See the engine for yourself") rather than the concrete output
the reader will receive from having just read 74-benchmark validation data.

Variant B replaces the third-card label with "See your bike against our 74
benchmarks" and the subhead with "Pick your bike. Get a quarter-mile ET from
the same physics you just read about." It names the specific output (your
bike's ET, grounded in the just-read 74-benchmark validation) on the click
target itself, and explicitly bridges the just-established technical
credibility into the first-run action.

Falsifiable: users finish the whitepaper and don't click `/simulator` because
the current CTA generic-mechanism phrasing fails to ground the just-read
technical credibility into a concrete first-run promise.

Distinct cohort from other live copy tests:
- EXP-008 (/features bottom CTA) targets mid-funnel evaluation readers.
- EXP-011 (/bikes/[id] top CTA) targets single-bike-locked-in browsers.
- EXP-013 targets the highest-intent technical-vetting cohort on the site —
  the only one for whom "against our 74 benchmarks" carries grounded specific
  meaning. EXP-012's decision-criteria block explicitly flagged this page as
  a EXP-013 candidate.

## Variants

| Arm | Button label | Subhead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (control) | Run a simulation → | See the engine for yourself. |
| B | See your bike against our 74 benchmarks → | Pick your bike. Get a quarter-mile ET from the same physics you just read about. |

Single variable: only the third card's label + subhead change. The two sibling
cards (Physics docs → `/docs/physics`, Validation data → `/docs/validation`),
the parent `<Link href="/simulator">`, the surrounding `<h2>Continue reading</h2>`,
the grid container, and the full 10-section article body are byte-identical
on both arms.

## Primary metric

`simulator_runs_per_whitepaper_visitor`

## Secondary metrics

- `whitepaper_terminal_cta_click_through_rate`
- `whitepaper_docs_physics_cta_ctr_delta` (cannibalisation guard)
- `whitepaper_docs_validation_cta_ctr_delta` (cannibalisation guard)
- `whitepaper_page_bounce_rate`
- `simulator_page_first_run_completion_rate_from_whitepaper_referrer`

## Decision criteria

Ship variant B as the new control if:
- `simulator_runs / whitepaper_visitor` lifts ≥15 % with p<0.05 over ≥200
  samples per arm, AND
- `simulator_page_first_run_completion_rate_from_whitepaper_referrer` does
  not drop >10 % vs. site-wide baseline (we want intent-to-run from the
  high-intent reader cohort, not curiosity-click inflation).

Roll back if:
- `whitepaper_page_bounce_rate` rises >10 % (verb-result copy reads out of
  place in the academic-document tone), OR
- `whitepaper_docs_physics_cta_ctr` OR `whitepaper_docs_validation_cta_ctr`
  drops >25 % in absolute terms — that means the third card is winning by
  cannibalising the two sibling cards rather than recruiting new clicks. The
  whitepaper footer is supposed to offer three legitimate next steps, not one
  dominant one.

No decision before 200 samples per arm.

## If B wins — follow-ups

Each as a SEPARATE experiment, not bundled:
- (a) Test verb-result variants on the two sibling cards (Physics docs →
  "Read the engine sub-model deep-dives" grounded equivalent, Validation data
  → "See the per-bike benchmark table"), one card at a time.
- (b) Consider promoting the technical-credibility-grounded copy pattern to
  `/docs/*` index pages which currently use similarly generic
  terminal-CTA wording.

## Implementation

- New file: `web/app/whitepaper/TerminalCtaVariant.js` (client component,
  `useSearchParams()` inside `<Suspense fallback={<Card variant="A" />}>` —
  fallback renders the control byte-for-byte so SSR/SSG markup matches
  pre-EXP-013 exactly. Hydration promotes to B only if `?variant=B` present).
- Wired into `web/app/whitepaper/page.js` by replacing the inline third
  `<Link href="/simulator">…</Link>` with `<TerminalCtaVariant />` and
  importing it at the top of the file.
- `data-exp="EXP-013"` + `data-variant` + `data-cta="whitepaper-terminal"`
  attributes on the `<Link>` itself so click-attribution survives without
  analytics infra.
- Meta tags (title, description, canonical `https://motoquant.in/whitepaper`,
  openGraph article), the cover, abstract, TOC, and all 10 SECTIONS are
  untouched — SEO-safe.
